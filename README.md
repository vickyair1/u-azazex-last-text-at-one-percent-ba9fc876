## The line

> I've been at 1% since lunch. I wanted to see how long you'd ignore me. Six hours. Goodbye — enjoy your brick.

109 characters, one line, in the dying phone's own voice.

## Why it works

The turn: the phone wasn't dying by accident — it was *faking* 1% since lunch to test its owner, and the owner failed the test for six hours. The kicker lands the phone's revenge: once it dies, the owner is left holding a brick. Specific details (since lunch, six hours), a consistent petty voice from first word to last, no wasted words, and no low-battery pun.

## How it runs

`main.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, asserting 1–199 characters, a single line, and valid UTF-8.
